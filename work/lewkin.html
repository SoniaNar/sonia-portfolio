LEWKIN x BAERRY

Lewkin is where Seoul street style meets the global Gen Z. Born from a love of fashion, culture, and creativity, Lewkin gives young people everywhere access to trend-driven looks and curated K-beauty too. Baerry is the dedicated K-beauty sister brand and platform of Lewkin that features popular Korean beauty brands.

As Global Influencer Marketer, I contacted 800+ beauty creators and partnered with 60+ across the Advent Calendar, Black Friday and Boxing Day campaigns, sourcing skincare, makeup and beauty creators on TikTok in the UK, US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand and vetting each by audience demographics to match target markets. I ran outreach and onboarding through an affiliate model — creators received product via gifting keys and earned commission through personalised discount codes — and managed draft orders and campaign tracking through Shopify and LEWKIN's internal platform.
