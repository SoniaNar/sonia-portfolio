Eeseol

EESEOL* launched a two-story K-beauty pop-up at The Grove, one of Los Angeles' highest-traffic retail destinations. The activation ("LIVE SEOUL") introduced more than 40 Korean beauty and lifestyle brands to the US market, aiming to build awareness and support distribution entry.

I led the recruitment and coordination of around 200 local creators for on-site presence at the event, aiming to support the launch of emerging brands and amplify organic reach.

*Seoul-born lifestyle platform operated by Lotte Home Shopping in partnership with KOTRA.
