LOEWE x Korea University Global MBA Consulting Project

As part of the Korea University Global MBA program, I served as Team Leader on a multicultural team of six to complete a three-month consulting project with LOEWE, a globally renowned Spanish Luxury Fashion brand.

LOEWE Korea's storytelling was not landing locally, resulting in low buzz, and missed opportunities in one of luxury's most high-impact markets. My team built localized strategies to bring LOEWE's art and craftsmanship heritage to life for a Korean audience, especially Millenials and GZs, with the goal of drawing 7,000+ visitors, engaging 300+ VIPs, and earning 10+ organic media features.
